Text marketing has become one of the most powerful ways for businesses to communicate with their customers. With open rates often exceeding 90%, text messaging allows businesses to reach their audience almost instantly. However, sending messages alone does not guarantee results. To truly understand the impact of a text campaign, businesses need to track how those messages translate into customer engagement, purchases, and long-term loyalty. Measuring the right metrics helps turn simple texts into measurable revenue.

One of the first indicators of text campaign success is engagement. While open rates for text messages are typically very high, businesses should focus on how customers interact with the message. This includes clicks on links, responses to promotions, or participation in offers. When customers actively engage with a text message, it shows that the message content, timing, and offer are resonating with them.

Another key metric is offer redemption. SMS coupons typically see 5 %–15 % redemption rates, and in some campaigns those rates surpass 30 %, making redemption tracking a strong indicator of performance. Redemption tracking allows businesses to see which promotions drive the most traffic and sales. Over time, this data helps businesses refine their offers and better understand what motivates their customers to take action.

Beyond engagement and redemptions, businesses should focus on return on investment (ROI). ROI measures how much revenue a campaign generates compared to the cost of running it. By tracking purchases tied to specific text campaigns, businesses can determine exactly how much revenue each message produces. This level of visibility allows businesses to confidently invest more in campaigns that are proven to drive sales.

Ultimately, measuring text campaign success comes down to connecting the dots between messages sent and revenue generated. By tracking engagement, redemption rates, and ROI, businesses can move beyond guesswork and make data-driven marketing decisions. When done correctly, text marketing becomes more than just a communication tool, it becomes a reliable and measurable driver of business growth.
