A text marketing and communication platform that helps businesses connect with customers and employees via SMS. It lets you send promotions, reminders, and automated messages to drive engagement and increase revenue.

  • Customers opt in via text, QR code, web form, or in-store kiosk.
  • You send messages like promotions, event reminders, or loyalty rewards.

Any business can use Customer Connect. It’s a particularly strong fit for:

  • Retail & restaurants — coupons, loyalty rewards
  • Salons & gyms — appointment reminders, promotions
  • Service businesses — customer updates, scheduling
  • Healthcare & dental offices — patient reminders
  • Corporate teams — employee alerts, shift updates
