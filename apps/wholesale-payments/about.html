Our story

From a Lubbock garage to all 50 states.

Mark Hodges founded what became Wholesale Payments out of his garage in 2007, providing equipment to local merchants. Those merchants kept asking for the same thing: a one-stop payment processing company with low rates, cutting-edge technology, and excellent customer service.

So Mark partnered with Greg Bernstein to build exactly that. Since then we've grown from six reps and three administrative staff in Lubbock, TX to eighty W2 employees and 300+ sales professionals across the country — while carrying our small-town values with us.
