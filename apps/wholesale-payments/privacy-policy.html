Wholesale Payments PRIVACY POLICY

Wholesale Payments (including, without limitation, affiliates, subsidiaries, partners, associates, divisions, agents, joint ventures, or other companies under common control with us which are collectively referred to herein as “WHOLESALE PAYMENTS”) respect the privacy of users of our websites (collectively, the “WHOLESALE PAYMENTS Websites”). WHOLESALE PAYMENTS’s privacy policy (this “Privacy Policy”) is intended to inform you of our policies, procedures and practices regarding the collection, use and disclosure of any information that we collect from users of the WHOLESALE PAYMENTS Websites as well as users of WHOLESALE PAYMENTS’s applications, products or services. This Privacy Policy does not apply to any websites, mobile applications or other online services that do not display or link to this Privacy Policy.

THE TYPES OF INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

We collect information from you in various ways when you access or use the WHOLESALE PAYMENTS Websites, including, without limitation, in the following ways:

(a) Personal Information Which We May Collect Via Information You Provide to Us

We may collect the information that you voluntarily provide to us. For example:

  • We may collect personal information from you, such as your first and last name, e-mail, telephone number and password when you create an account.
  • If you tell us where you are (e.g., by allowing your mobile device to send us your location or by entering your city, state and/or zip code information), we may use that information to provide you with location-based information, such as the location of restaurants in your proximity that use Wholesale Payments’ applications, products or services. If you want to deactivate this feature, you can deactivate GPS on your mobile device.
  • If you utilize our applications, products or services which allow you to store preferences such as your location, recent orders and favorite menu items in order to make it more convenient for you to place additional orders in the future, we may collect that information.
  • If you order menu items from Wholesale Payments’s Websites or from third-party websites that use Wholesale Payments’s applications, products or services, we may collect information which we believe is necessary to complete the transaction, including, without limitation, your name and address.
  • If you provide us feedback or contact us via email, we may collect your name and email address, as well as any other content included in the email.
  • We may also collect other types of personal information that you provide to us voluntarily, such as any information requested by Wholesale Payments if you contact us regarding information about or support requests for any Wholesale Payments applications, products or services.

(b) Personal Information Which We May Collect Via Technology

We may collect certain information from you automatically as described below when you access and use the WHOLESALE PAYMENTS Websites or WHOLESALE PAYMENTS’s applications, products or services:

  • Information Collected by Wholesale Payments’s Servers. To make the Wholesale Payments Websites and Wholesale Payments’s applications, products and services more useful to you, our servers (which may be hosted by a third-party service provider) may collect information from you, including the referring/exit pages, your browser type, domain name, date/time stamp of your visit and your Internet Protocol (IP) address (a number that is automatically assigned to your computer or device when you use the Internet). We use this information to analyze trends, administer and update the Wholesale Payments Websites and Wholesale Payments’s applications, products and services, to monitor for fraudulent activities and for the efficacy of our fraud management tools, and to better tailor our applications, products and services to our users’ needs.
  • Cookies. Like many companies with websites, we use cookies to collect information. “Cookies” are small pieces of information that a web site sends to your computer’s hard drive while you are viewing a web site. We may use both session cookies (which expire once you close your web browser) and persistent cookies (which stay on your computer until you delete them) to provide you with a more personal and interactive experience on the Wholesale Payments Websites. This type of information is collected to make the Wholesale Payments Websites as well as Wholesale Payments’s applications, products and services more useful to you and to tailor your experience with us to meet your particular interests and needs.
  • Mobile Services. We may also collect non-personal information from your mobile device if you have downloaded Wholesale Payments’s mobile application. This information is generally used to help us deliver the most relevant information to you. Examples of information that may be collected and used include, without limitation, your geographic location, how you use the Wholesale Payments application(s), and information about the type of mobile device you use. In addition, in the event Wholesale Payments’s application crashes your mobile device, we may receive information about your mobile device model software version and the device carrier, which allow us to identify and fix bugs and otherwise improve performance of Wholesale Payments’s application(s). This information is sent to us as aggregated information and is not traceable to any individual and cannot be used to identify an individual.
  • Third Party Analytics. We may use third party analytics service providers to help analyze how users use the Wholesale Payments Websites and Wholesale Payments’s applications, products and services. These analytics service providers may use Cookies to collect information such as how often users visit the Wholesale Payments Websites, what pages they visit and what other websites they used prior to coming to the Wholesale Payments Websites. We may use the information we get from these analytics service providers to improve the Wholesale Payments Websites and Wholesale Payments’s applications, products or services.

(c) Social Networking Websites and Services

The WHOLESALE PAYMENTS Websites and WHOLESALE PAYMENTS’s applications, products and services may allow users to access a social networking sites (each, an “SNS”) to interact with and share with their friends their experiences with WHOLESALE PAYMENTS or company’s which utilize WHOLESALE PAYMENTS’s applications, products or services. If you use an SNS that WHOLESALE PAYMENTS supports, and then entering your SNS login credentials on such SNS we may receive information from that SNS to make it easier for you to create an account with WHOLESALE PAYMENTS and to show you our relevant content on such SNS. Any information that we collect from each of your SNS account may depend on the privacy settings you have with such SNS, so please consult your SNS’s privacy and data practices.

(d) Location Information.

If you have enabled location services on your mobile device and agree to the collection of your location when prompted by the WHOLESALE PAYMENTS Websites or by WHOLESALE PAYMENTS’s applications, products or services, we may collect your location information in order to locate those restaurants or other businesses which are in proximity to you and which utilize WHOLESALE PAYMENTS’s applications, products or services. If you do not want us to collect this information collected, you may decline the collection of your location when prompted or disable location services on your mobile device.

In general, we use your personal information to respond to your requests as submitted through the WHOLESALE PAYMENTS Websites or through WHOLESALE PAYMENTS’s applications, products and services, to provide you with the information, products and services that you request, or to aid us in serving your better. (b) Creation of Anonymous Information

We may create anonymous information records from personal information by excluding information (such as your name) that make the information personally identifiable to you. We reserve the right to use and disclose anonymous Information to third parties in our sole discretion.
