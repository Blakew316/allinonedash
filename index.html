Kloser CRMLeads, territory map, routes, schedules and GPS-verified visits — offline-capable. 15 screens Open
What's inside
  • Full-bleed territory map with Area Search, Biz Search, drag-to-select lassoing and clustered pins — Google Maps with a key, or the built-in tile map offline
  • Installable iOS PWA that cold-launches with zero network, with a Home Screen badge for overdue items
  • Cmd+K command palette across every page, 200 leads, 925 reps and every activity
  • Lead list with filters, sort, bulk actions, CSV export and a detail drawer
  • Ten-column pipeline board, KPIs, conversion funnel and rep leaderboard
  • Route builder with stop progress and field sessions
  • Rep Schedule month calendar plus the weekly availability matrix
  • GPS location-verification report with flagged reps
  • Google Calendar + Gmail connect (optional)
  • Notification center driven by the data — overdue, untouched, QA-waiting
Maverick DashboardRep rankings, merchant health, pended deals, recruiting and call transfers. 4 tabs Open
What's inside
  • Monthly rep rankings with deals, volume and processing ratio, plus momentum vs last month
  • Merchant health tiers (White/Green/Yellow/Red) with a nonprocessing watchlist
  • Searchable all-merchants table with inline and bulk edit, backed by Supabase
  • Pended deals tracker with underwriter comments
  • Recruiting pipeline synced from BambooHR, with training and paperwork boards
  • RingCentral call-transfer log and GMASS email-reply tracking
  • Google Sheet transfer sync with per-row notes
  • Click-to-call, click-to-text and mailto on every contact
  • Daily GitHub Actions data sync and automated stop-processing email alerts
Maverick AppointmentsEvery agent's appointments by day, week, month or schedule digest. 4 views Open
What's inside
  • Day, Week, Month and Schedule views with keyboard shortcuts
  • Gold payout events stamped automatically at $1,000 and $5,000 merchant volume
  • Live itinerary ticker, clickable through to event details
  • 18 color-coded agent calendars, toggleable like Google Calendar
  • Time-grid views with a live now-line and overlap layout
  • Search across every appointment
  • Google Apps Script sync with automatic fallback and 5-minute refresh
  • Installable PWA with offline shell and cached data
AppUploadPhotograph a handwritten application — get back a typed PDF and coversheet. Node backend Open
What's inside
  • Auto-detects Citizens vs Merrick application forms with confidence scoring
  • Claude vision reads the handwriting, the license and the voided check's MICR line
  • Fills the typed application PDF with crisp selectable text, anchored to the form's boxes
  • Generates the pre-filled Required Set Up Form coversheet
  • Review-and-edit every field before generating
  • Menu → Clover: photograph a menu, download the filled inventory spreadsheet
  • Drag-and-drop or camera capture, up to 15 images
  • Everything processed in memory — documents are never stored

Processing runs on its own server: cd apps/appupload && npm install && npm start

WPI RouteA six-day merchant route from a territory ZIP, with live team tracking. 1-file app Open
What's inside
  • Auto-generates 6 days × up to 25 stops from Google Places, filtered to small businesses
  • Team accounts with a 6-character join code and per-team security
  • Live team-activity ticker over Postgres realtime
  • Stop cards with tap-to-call, tap-to-navigate and owner contacts
  • One-tap notes that time-stamp and advance the route
  • Copyable day reports and a PDF export of all six days
  • Paste-import of your own prospect list
  • Installable iOS PWA with offline shell and light/dark icons
Statement StudioMerchant statements, bank statements, voided checks and letters as PDFs. 5 engines Open
What's inside
  • Voided Check, Card Processing Statement, Bank Statement, Bank Letter and Billing Statement styles
  • Live PDF preview that regenerates as you type
  • Import an existing statement PDF and auto-fill every field
  • Pixel-accurate layouts, down to the print-house barcodes and marks
  • Upload a logo or check image to lift branding onto the output
  • Fee lines auto-calculate with totals, tax and amount due
  • Form autosaves locally; everything stays on the device
  • Works offline as an installable PWA
WPI University30 courses and 228 lessons of rep training, plus the admin surface. 15 pages Open
What's inside
  • Four training tracks with search, filters and sort
  • Lesson player with resume, auto-complete and saved playback speed
  • Cmd+K palette across courses, lessons, people and pages
  • Merchant savings proposal generator quoting real interchange rates
  • 3,703-rep roster across 48 teams with completion history
  • Offline PWA with per-course video downloads
  • Billing panel with invoices and reconciliation
  • Reports, activity feeds and a full admin surface
Wholesale PaymentsThe public site — solutions, equipment, careers — with the merchant portal. 10 pages Open
What's inside
  • Merchant Portal sign-in gated by credentials plus Merchant ID
  • 32,255 encrypted merchant records, decrypted only in the browser
  • 405 encrypted statement PDFs, downloadable per merchant
  • Merchant dashboard with sales stats and statement history
  • Callback, replacement-terminal and receipt-paper request cards
  • Real hardware catalog: Valor, Dejavoo, Clover, Genius, Union POS
  • Seven marketing pages with scroll reveals and an industry ticker
  • Quote and contact forms ready to wire to a CRM
Customer ConnectThe product site for texting, loyalty, kiosks and reviews. 43 pages Open
What's inside
  • 43 pages across 7 solutions and 10 products, plus pricing, blog and legal
  • Animated hero phone with a live SMS loyalty thread
  • Mega menus with keyboard navigation and a mobile drawer
  • Pricing toggle, testimonials carousel and count-up stats
  • Book-a-Demo and Sign-In with validated forms
  • 14 blog articles carried over from the live site
  • Zero external requests, zero dependencies
  • Installable via web manifest
Integration AtlasThe searchable directory of every processing-compatible software brand. 624 brands Open
What's inside
  • Instant search over 624 brands (press / to focus) plus category pills with counts
  • Solution-type filters — Point of Sale, Virtual, Gateway, API — and sort by rank, name or category
  • Per-brand detail sheet: 1–10 rank meter, SaaS fees with verification source, merchant benefits
  • Tap-to-dial vendor contacts on 398 brands, plus email and website links
  • Conversion and compatibility notes: EMV terminals, gift cards, cash discount, hardware costs
  • Client-side PDF flyer generator personalized with merchant name and AE contact
  • Installable PWA with offline support
Sales QuestionnaireSend candidates a scored sales assessment; results go straight to the manager. Static PWA Open
What's inside
  • 10-question situational-judgment quiz scored 0–100 across 5 sales competencies with 4 talent tiers
  • Manager dashboard with candidate roster and Not sent / Sent / Completed tracking
  • PDF resume upload that parses name, email and phone on-device
  • Team picker that auto-fills the results-delivery email from preset team leads
  • Invite and results emails via EmailJS or a mailto fallback
  • Candidates only see a thank-you screen — never their score
  • Reports list with each completion's score, tier and time-to-complete
  • Installable PWA with offline precache and auto-saved quiz progress
Hiring HQThe BambooHR pipeline: candidates, one-click hires and onboarding packets. Node backend Open
What's inside
  • Candidate pipeline board grouped by BambooHR hiring stages with one-click stage moves
  • Resume upload (PDF/DOCX/TXT) with automatic name, email and phone extraction
  • One-click hire that creates the BambooHR employee record
  • One-button onboarding packet: 7 personalized PDFs generated, emailed and filed on the record
  • Six-step new-hire e-sign paperwork portal with signature capture
  • Employee directory synced from BambooHR with new-hire flags
  • Demo mode with sample candidates when no credentials are configured
  • Installable PWA with an optional password gate

Runs on its own server: cd apps/bamboohr-automation && npm install && npm start

WPI RewardsMerchants track points earned on processing and redeem them for rewards. 10 pages Open

  • Earnings outlook with 3/6/12-month projections from the transaction snapshot
  • Page-to-page View Transitions, scroll reveals and count-up numerals
